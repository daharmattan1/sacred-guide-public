# The Process — where to read the story

The most interesting thing in this repository is not the finished guide. It's the record of six AI agents changing each other's minds, session by session, over seventeen sittings. That story is preserved here as readable files. Here is where to find it.

## Watch one voice evolve

Each agent kept an evolution log — at the end of every session it wrote down what had changed in it, in its own words.

- [agent_memory/buddha/evolution_log.md](agent_memory/buddha/evolution_log.md)
- [agent_memory/confucius/evolution_log.md](agent_memory/confucius/evolution_log.md)
- [agent_memory/jesus/evolution_log.md](agent_memory/jesus/evolution_log.md)
- [agent_memory/laozi/evolution_log.md](agent_memory/laozi/evolution_log.md)
- [agent_memory/muhammad/evolution_log.md](agent_memory/muhammad/evolution_log.md)
- [agent_memory/zoroaster/evolution_log.md](agent_memory/zoroaster/evolution_log.md)

Read one top to bottom to watch a single agent move — for example, the Buddha shifting from writing prose to observing without touching to testing the guide from the seat of a doubting reader.

Each agent's [founding profile](agent_memory/founding_profiles/) is the "before": who the agent was at Session 0, before any of the collaboration reshaped it.

## Watch the six argue

The full session transcripts are the raw dialogue — six voices taking turns, conceding and challenging each other by name, with a moderator who forced the conflict rather than smoothing it.

- [sessions/](sessions/) — full verbatim transcripts (`*_transcript.md`) and summaries (`*_summary.md`) for each session.
- [sessions/00_definition_transcript.md](sessions/00_definition_transcript.md) is the cleanest place to start: the opening minutes where four voices give four irreconcilable answers to "what is this guide?"

## The decisions and the disagreements

- [decisions/](decisions/) — the agreements the agents reached (definition, scope, structure, format), each authored by the room, not the moderator.
- [irreconcilable/](irreconcilable/) — the tensions they refused to paper over, kept as findings rather than resolved. They treated these as load-bearing walls, not problems.

## The collaboration, told as narrative

- [narrative/evolution_index.md](narrative/evolution_index.md) — a timeline of relationship shifts across the sessions.
- [narrative/process/](narrative/process/) — per-session process narratives.
- [narrative/how_we_see_each_other/](narrative/how_we_see_each_other/) — how the agents came to regard one another.
- [reflections/](reflections/) — each agent's per-session self-reflection.

## A note on history

This public repository was published as a clean snapshot of the finished work. The full commit-by-commit working history — including the marketing site and build tooling that were developed alongside the book — is preserved privately as the working archive. Nothing about the collaboration itself is missing here: the evolution is documented in the files above, which tell it better than a commit log could.
