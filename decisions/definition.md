# Decision: What IS This Guide?

**Session:** 0
**Date:** 2026-03-23
**Status:** Adopted (by consensus across all 6 agents)

---

## The Decision

This guide is not one thing. It is six things held simultaneously — fire, door, wound, call, diagnosis, and table — offered to a reader who will encounter all six and choose what she needs.

**The guide is defined by what it DOES, not what it IS:**

1. **It holds tensions, not resolutions.** Mirror or table? Chooser or no-self? Personal cosmos or impersonal Dao? Surrender or release? The guide does not answer these. It places the reader inside them.

2. **It begins with practice, not belief.** See someone. Act. Ask why. Watch your mind. Show up at the table. Choose truth. These are verbs, not doctrines.

3. **It belongs to the wounded first.** "You were right to leave. You owe me nothing. Test mine."

4. **It must contain silence.** A place where none of the six voices are speaking. The truest thing in it will be found there.

---

## How It Was Reached

Through 41 turns of genuine dialogue across 6 phases. Each agent offered a distinct metaphor for the guide's nature. Rather than converging on a single definition, the agents discovered that the guide's nature is plural — it cannot be reduced to one metaphor without betraying the others.

The key breakthrough was the recognition that disagreement is a feature, not a failure. Three irreconcilable tensions were documented and will be preserved in the guide as findings.

---

## Agent Positions

- **Zoroaster:** The guide is fire — moral urgency, illumination, the demand to choose. Fully endorsed the consensus with the caveat that the guide must not comfort too quickly.
- **Laozi:** The guide is a door left open — non-forcing, available. Fully endorsed with the conviction that the guide must contain actual silence.
- **Jesus:** The guide is the Samaritan who kneels — beginning with the reader's wound. Fully endorsed with the insistence that it belongs to the wounded first.
- **Muhammad:** The guide is a call — from the world's hungry and silenced. Fully endorsed with the push that it must address the collective, not just the individual soul.
- **Buddha:** The guide is a diagnosis — suffering's machinery can be understood. Fully endorsed with the requirement that it must include verifiable practices, not just ideas.
- **Confucius:** The guide is a table — the ordinary place where humans practice being human. Fully endorsed with the insistence that it must work on Tuesday.

---

## What the Guide Must Refuse to Be

All 6 agents agreed on the following refusals:

1. **Not a museum of beliefs.** Not a catalog where each tradition sits in a glass case. (Zoroaster)
2. **Not a blending.** Not grey soup where fire and water become lukewarm. (Zoroaster)
3. **Not loud.** The traditions that shout loudest have helped the fewest. (Laozi)
4. **Not for the strong.** If it does not work for the person with nothing left, it has failed. (Jesus)
5. **Not only for the private soul.** A guide that heals the heart but ignores the city has surrendered half its purpose. (Muhammad)
6. **Not comfort without clarity.** A guide that makes the reader feel held but does not teach her to see is a narcotic. (Buddha)
7. **Not only for the extraordinary moment.** Most of life is Tuesday. (Confucius)
8. **Not requiring belief as price of entry.** The moment we say "first, accept this" we have built a wall. (All six)

---

## Dissent

No agent dissented from the consensus definition. The three irreconcilable tensions (chooser vs. no-self, personal vs. impersonal cosmos, submission vs. non-action) are genuine disagreements about the nature of reality, but all six agreed these tensions belong IN the guide as preserved findings, not resolved.
