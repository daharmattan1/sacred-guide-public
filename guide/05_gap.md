The shoes are where he left them. The light has moved across the floor since you sat down. You have been here awhile.

Nothing is asked of you. Not by this guide, not by the morning, not by the shoes. You are allowed to stay here. You are allowed to leave and come back. You are allowed to not come back.

The fire did not ask your permission. You do not owe it an answer.
