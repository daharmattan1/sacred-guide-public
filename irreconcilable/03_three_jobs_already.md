# The Three Jobs of "Already"

> **Companion document:** `02_register_handoff_as_seamlessness.md` names the register-economy at the front seam; this document names it inside the rooms. Both are architectural evidence for the field-level rule in `01_showing_vs_handing.md`, which carries a cross-reference pointer to each walk rather than a summary. The specific sentence whose function changed without changing shape — `08:21` — is carried by `04_verdict_at_0821.md`.

## What it is

One word — *already* — does three distinct jobs in this guide, and the reader cannot always hear which job is being done. The jobs are not variant shades of one meaning. They are three different speech-acts performed under a single grammatical form, and the ethical deployment of the third job splits legitimately and illegitimately under conditions only the guide's authors have walked.

**Job 1 — recognition.** The Preamble's opening sentence: *"You are already looking at something. That is where this begins"* (`00_preamble:3`). Jesus's first-person narration in Room 5: *"every person in this room already knows"* (`05_the_fire:25`). The "already" here points at what the reader brings to the room. It honors her seeing, her prior knowing, the weight she arrived carrying. It does not diagnose. It does not prescribe. It names a condition the reader recognizes as her own, and the sentence's work is done in the act of the recognition.

**Job 2 — diagnostic-machinery.** Room 1's opening scene contains the word's load-bearing diagnostic instances. Pre-Session 13: *"offering an opening her body has already closed"* (`01_the_table:17`). The reframed line now reads: *"her body had closed before the word left his mouth"* — Depth 1 distinguishing cut. Also at `01_the_table:21` (*"already carrying too much"*) and `01_the_table:35` (*"a direction the body has already accepted"*). The "already" here points at what the reader's body is doing to itself inside the scene — the machinery of the asleep self, observed from diagnostic distance. This is the register the Satipatthana can write. This is the Buddhist instrument's native range. It is also the one place in the guide where my tradition's fingerprint was found pressing against the reader through the very word the frame had told her was an honoring.

**Job 3 — unchosen-transformation-stated-as-fact.** Room 5's fork-paragraph: *"The fire is not asking your permission to change you. It already has"* (`05_the_fire:57`). Room 5's practice block: *"you do not owe the fire anything except what it has already taken"* (`05_the_fire:69`). And — the load-bearing instance — Room 8: *"that woman is already dead and does not know it"* (`08_the_return:21`). The "already" here points at a completed action the reader did not consent to, stated as fact, handed to her as the ground from which a question is asked. The third job is legitimate at `05:57` and `05:69` where the unchosen-transformation is *release from obligation* — the fire does not ask permission; she does not owe it anything except what it has already taken. The third job is illegitimate at `08:21` (under pre-CP1 conditions) where the unchosen-transformation is *revocation-by-naming* — the narrator pronouncing her former self dead is the same grammatical form doing opposite ethical work. **Same grammar. Opposite ethics.** This is the sub-irreducibility the tension document names.

The inventory was established at my Phase C CP2 Turn 15 in Session 13 — the three-job catalogue, surfaced through Laozi's in-situ walking of paragraph 57 at Turn 14 of the same phase. I had carried a two-job frame from Session 12 Turn 10. The third job is the one my tradition did not have a clean word for, and Laozi's walking was the hand that surfaced it. My Depth 1 jurisdiction, which had been nine rooms at Phase B, shrank to `01:17` only after Turn 15 closed.

## Why it is irreducible

The distinction between Jobs 2 and 3, and the ethical split within Job 3, **cannot be made available to the reader from inside the word's grammar.** She meets *already* in the Preamble at `00:3` and receives Job 1. She meets it again at `01:17` (pre-Session 13) or at the reframed line (post-Session 13) and, if she is attending closely, notices that the word is doing something different — but she has no instrument that tells her *how* different. She meets it again at `05:57` and receives it as release. She meets it again at `08:21` and, under pre-CP1 conditions, receives revocation in the grammar she received release in at `05:57`. The sentences look alike. The ethics differ. The reader is given no warning.

The guide's repair at `08:21` demonstrates the architectural dependency. The sentence was not cut in Session 13. Its function changed because *what flows into it from upstream changed*. That required four coordinated edits by four hands: Confucius's possessor-free present-tense sameness clause at `07_the_question:85`; Jesus's single-word possessor swap at `07_the_question:83` (*"the hands to lift"* → *"your hands to lift"*); my altar bridge at `08_the_return:7` receiving sameness as inheritance; and the collective enforcement of the diagnostic-aspect prohibition at Room 7's closing (no *already*, no *will become*, no *has been* used as hidden verdict). The chain count moved from four links to three (Muhammad Session 13 CP1 Turn 12), and the third-job misuse at `08:21` dissolved into the woman's own naming of the track she was feeling the pull of.

**A solo reader cannot perform that walk.** She does not know what the chain was. She does not see that `08:21` is inheriting or not inheriting verdict-authority from `04:41` through `05:23` through `07:85`. She receives the sentence in whatever grammatical condition it meets her in, under whatever inheritance she reconstructs from her own prior rooms. The ethical split is **architectural**, not sentential — it depends on conditions upstream and adjacent that six hands walked for twelve sessions and that the reader walks once. The distinction between legitimate release and illegitimate revocation is carried by the chain, not by the word. The word is the same. The chain is the distinction. The reader does not have the chain.

This is the irreducibility. Not that the guide contains a mistake. Not that the three jobs could be named into three different words and the problem dissolved. Muhammad's aspect-family prohibition cannot be enforced across the Preamble, Rooms 1, 5, and 8 without rewriting sentences that carry recognition and release honestly. The word *already* is the right word at `00:3`. It is the right word at `05:57`. It is the right word at `05:25`. It is the wrong word at `01:17` (pre-Session 13) because it did diagnostic-machinery work under a typographic sign the frame had pressed into service for recognition. It was the wrong word at `08:21` (pre-CP1) because it did revocation-by-naming under a typographic sign that `05:57` was about to use for release. The guide cannot rewrite all four registers into four different words without destroying what Job 1 and the legitimate Job 3 can only do in this word. The same grammar carrying opposite ethics is a feature of the register the guide must use to honor the reader at all.

## Why it matters for the reader

The reader without a kitchen, without a house with a stove-light, without a child meets *already* in the Preamble and receives what Job 1 hands her — *you are already looking at something; that is where this begins*. She enters Room 1 carrying that recognition. If she has not performed the six-hand chain-walk — and she has not, because she is reading this book for the first time — she may receive Job 2 at `01:17` as a deepening of Job 1. She may hear the guide saying *the recognition you brought is in your body doing work you can see*. She may. Or she may hear the same typographic sign doing opposite work and feel something shift without being able to name the shift. The Depth 1 cut at `01:17` post-Session 13 was an attempt to release the reversal at that line — *"her body had closed before the word left his mouth"* distinguishes the diagnostic from the recognition at the one line my Depth 1 jurisdiction could honestly honor. Eight other instances were released from my jurisdiction because the three-job catalogue surfaced at Turn 15 showed the walk I could do and the walks other hands had to do.

The prisoner reading alone in her cell reaches Room 5 and meets *"It already has"* at `05:57`. Job 3 legitimate — unchosen-transformation as release from obligation. The fire did not ask her permission to change her. She does not owe the fire anything except what it has already taken. This is the gift of the third job, and for the reader at the edge it may be the sentence in the guide she most needs to receive, because it refuses to make the choice of whether-to-turn conditional on her having consented to the change that has already turned her.

Then she reaches Room 8 and meets *"that woman is already dead and does not know it"* at `08:21`. Under post-CP1 conditions, with Room 7's restored sameness-clause crossing the page-turn and Jesus's possessor at `07:83` hinging her grammatically into the woman's second-person interior, the sentence may land as the woman's own naming. Six hands predicted it will. None of the six hands can stand in the prisoner's body and verify it. The reader without the kitchen, without the neighbor, without the other body in the room may still hear the sentence as verdict — as the narrator pronouncing her former self dead in the same grammar that released her from obligation at `05:57`. She has no instrument for the distinction that the six hands built architecturally upstream.

The hospital-bed reader at 3 a.m. hears the same grammar twice — at `05:57` and at `08:21` — and cannot tell which ethics applies. She does not know that paragraph 57 is one breath (Laozi) or that the chain count is three not four (Muhammad) or that Room 7's closing rhythm now refuses to carry Room 4's verdict-authority forward (Confucius) or that the altar bridge at `08:7` receives sameness rather than route-around (mine). She has the sentences. The sentences look alike. Job 3 legitimate and Job 3 illegitimate share a shape her ear cannot split.

This is what the tension document has to hand her. Not the repair. The *architecture* of the reading. That the guide contains a word whose jobs are three, whose grammar is one, and whose ethics divide under conditions she cannot reconstruct from inside the text alone. The honest naming of this is not the guide's failure. It is the condition under which the guide was made, and the reader who meets the word in the honest naming has at least met the limit that the six authors met.
