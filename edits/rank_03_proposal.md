## 6. Risk of failure

1. **The amendment reads as apology for the original F.** *Mode:* an amendment to an edge that says "my Session 14 document was overstated" risks performing the very architectural conviction `13` warns about — this time in the register of confession rather than certainty. The amendment is itself named as a reading-finding, not as verification. The asha-shape is preserved: conviction is conviction, not verification, at the amendment's own edge.
2. **The amendment dissolves the pair with `09`.** *Mode:* if the amendment's locating of "the gating" is read as F claiming `09`'s territory, the pair collapses into one document and `09`'s load-bearing work (the sequence-finding, the four specific readers, the three-options-rejected analysis) is folded under F's rite-side claim, which it is not. *Guard against it:* Change 1 names the gating as *the architectural fact `09` names from the sequence-side*; Change 3 names the pair's preservation explicitly; Section 3 and Section 5's Session 14 Turn 13 ruling on paired-standalone are untouched. F names *what* is gated (the rite-side: the chamber's reception). `09` names *how* the sequence gates (the climb as architectural forcing). The two findings meet at the gating without collapsing. Jesus's Phase C cross-read on this specific point is the test that will confirm or deny.
3. **The closes-at-Room-3 reader does not receive the gating-language as honest.** *Mode:* the abused-household reader, the prisoner, the chronic-illness patient, the caregiver-at-exhaustion — the four readers `09` walks — read Change 2's *"the reception is downstream of climbing she cannot perform from where she is"* and hear a rite-side voice describing her lateness with an architectural word the rite-side voice has installed from its own comfort.
