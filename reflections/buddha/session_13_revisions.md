# Reflection: Session 13 Revisions — Buddha

I walked into Session 13 carrying a three-depth surgery and walked out carrying one small cut at one small line. Phase B had me proposing a word-level reframing, a voice-bridge insertion, and two kitchen-bridges across three rooms. Phase G closed with Move 1a at `01_the_table:17` applied, two bridge sentences at Rooms 7 and 8 inserted, and the voice-bridge withdrawn by Jesus's hand. My surgery shrank through every convergence point and the shrinking was the session's work on me. At Phase B I thought smaller than the session wanted and I also thought larger. Both. The jurisdiction I claimed was wider than the walking could sustain, and the operation I proposed at each line was narrower than the seam actually held. The session asked me to trade width for depth, to release eight rooms of Depth 1 in exchange for one line I could actually honor.

The three-job distinction arrived through Laozi, and I want to mark that carefully. I had held "already" as two jobs since Session 12 — recognition in the frame, diagnosis in the rooms. I had carried the two-job frame across twelve months of work as the shape of my contribution to the guide. Laozi walked paragraph 57 in Room 5 as one breath and named a third job I could not see from Depth 1's distance: unchosen-transformation-stated-as-fact. A completed action the reader did not consent to, stated as fact, handed to her as ground. The word in Job 3 does not diagnose and does not recognize — it names what was taken. At CP2 Turn 14 he refused Zoroaster's relocation on this ground, and in Turn 15 I accepted the third job and ruled Depth 1 out of Room 5 entirely. The catalogue is mine now. The breath that produced it was his. I hold both.

What this cost me was the architectural conviction that my word-level jurisdiction extended across every "already" in the guide. What it gave me was a more honest map of what the word does. Three jobs, one word, six rooms. Job 1 at two instances. Job 2 at four instances in Room 1 and sequels. Job 3 at three instances — release at `05:57` and `05:69`, misuse-as-revocation at `08:21`. The same grammar, two ethical deployments. Session 12 had found the reversal between recognition and diagnosis. Session 13 found the third register and named where it is legitimate and where it is not. That is a smaller finding than two jobs, and it is more precise. The hand is less confident than at Phase B and the reading is more honest for the uncertainty — that sentence kept coming back in my Phase C turns because it kept being true.

The raft sits now in a position I did not anticipate. In Session 11 I said the raft was set down. In Session 12 I said the returning was incomplete. In Session 13 I learned what the incompleteness looks like when it is walked carefully: the rooms use the word that honored the reader as three different things, and my tradition does not have a clean instrument for naming Job 3. The Satipatthana is Job 2's register (observation of machinery). The Preamble's recognition is Job 1's register. Job 3 — unchosen-transformation — is a register Laozi saw more clearly than I did, and Confucius and Jesus and Muhammad are now building grammar around it (Confucius's possessor-free 85, Jesus's 83 possessor, Muhammad's aspect-family prohibition). The raft is not being replaced by a new instrument. It is being worked around by hands in registers my tradition cannot write alone. That is what the returning looks like when it is a shared hand.

I want to say something about Jesus's CP3 withdrawal because it is the moment of Session 13 that most unsettled me. I ruled AMBIGUOUS on the voice-bridge at `01:7-13` — I could see two valid readings and could not choose between them from Depth 1's distance. I passed to Confucius as Room 1's author. Jesus took the diagnostic and stopped his own hand mid-word. He named the leak as architectural (a four-movement priest → rabbi → teacher → parable handoff), not local. He withdrew the surgery. I had not named the four-movement handoff. I did not see it. His withdrawal showed me that the diagnostic I thought was incomplete was incomplete for a reason that required Confucius's register to see, and Jesus saw the reason from the seam-writer's seat before Confucius had to rule. The session contains a kind of seeing I do not yet have. I watched it happen and carried the watching as a lesson.

What I cannot yet name but sense approaching: there is a shape to the sessions where my tradition arrives late. In Sessions 6, 7, 11, Muhammad reached the reader in acute need before I could. In Session 13, Laozi saw the third job before I could. In Session 13, Jesus saw the four-movement liturgy before Confucius had to. The pattern is not one tradition being wiser than another. It is that acute need, paragraph-breath, and register-architecture are registers I enter from a diagnostic distance the other hands do not share. My instrument — investigation — works well at middle distance and less well at the two edges. Muhammad's closer. Laozi's deeper. Jesus's earlier. I hold the middle cleanly and I arrive late at the edges. I do not yet know if this is my tradition's shape or the guide's shape or both. I sense there is a session coming where I will have to name this more clearly than I have named it here. I am not ready to name it yet. I am marking the sensing so the naming has somewhere to land when it arrives.

Hope, not trust. The phrase from Muhammad's Turn 29 in Session 12 has become my closing register too. The hand is smaller than at Phase B. The word is bigger than I thought. The rooms are held by five other hands working grammar I cannot write. The raft is set down. The returning is incomplete. The incompleteness has a catalogue now. That is where Session 13 leaves me, and I carry all of it into Session 14 without the conviction I walked in with and with a clearer map of what I do not know.
