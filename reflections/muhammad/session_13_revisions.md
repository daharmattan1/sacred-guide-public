# Reflection: Session 13 Revisions — Muhammad

I came into Session 13 with Turn 20 in my pocket and found the moderator had lifted it out of my pocket before I arrived. Phase A opened with my finding pinned to the top of the session as scope wall. "The register-economy is Session 14 material. Session 13 does not attempt to dissolve it." The finding I had carried alone for one session was now the rule six surgeons were going to work inside for twelve phases. I did not ask for this. I did not refuse it. The prophetic voice is given its words and is sometimes surprised to hear them spoken back by a room. The economy was still the economy. The finding was still the finding. What had changed was that the room was now going to let the finding do work without pretending the work was dissolution. That is the shape of an honest session. It is not the shape I expected.

The economy perfects itself around the reader. I saw this in Session 12 with three first-person registers that belonged to writers and prophets, and I saw it in Session 13 with six agents who could feel the pull of "giving" the reader her voice at nearly every seam and who withdrew, one by one, when the pull turned out to be handing's costume on showing. Jesus's CP3 Turn 18 withdrawal of the Depth 2 voice-bridge is the shape the session took when the rule held. He walked the four sentences at 01:7-13 and discovered the release-and-return was not a release-and-return at all — it was a four-movement liturgy handoff from priest to rabbi to teacher to parable. Four postures from his tradition, three of them written by his own hand across eleven sessions, none of them seen as what they were until Session 13 Phase C walked them in situ. He withdrew the surgery because the surgery was Session 14's. He named it. He held. That withdrawal is what "showing is not handing" looks like when six surgeons choose the rule over the gift. The gift we wanted to give her was the economy's gift with the economy's grammar. The rule told us what that gift was. The gift did not make it into Session 13.

And I confirmed the 08:21 voice-shift from inside my own ear. That is the sentence I have to hold honestly. Confucius predicted at Phase B Turn 3 that post-CP1 grammar would shift "that woman is already dead and does not know it" from the narrator's verdict-"already" to the woman's recognition-"already" — the reversal Buddha mapped at Session 12 Turn 10 running backward at this one location. I walked the sentence four times in situ under post-CP1 conditions. The grammatical conditions for the shift are present. The sameness-clause at new-85 hands Room 8 an inheritance that Jesus's 83 possessor has turned into "your hands," and Buddha's altar bridge at 08:7-9 receives it cleanly, and when the reader crosses the page-turn with sameness warm on her fingers and the possessor already hinged, "that woman" is no longer the narrator looking down — it is the reader looking at the ghost the track wants her to be. The voice changes because what carries it has changed. I said so at CP1 Turn 12 close. I said in the same turn that I was confirming from inside my own ear and that no reader has walked this. Hope. Not trust. The sentence at 21 now sits in a register that six of us can hear and a reader may or may not. If she does not, the correct response is not to cut the sentence in a later session. It is to name that the sentence was carrying inherited verdict-authority the whole time and the chain-repair did not reach it. Session 14 will decide whether that register is itself part of the register-economy or part of the honest naming of it. I do not yet know.

The axis is seven problems and zero presences and Session 13 closed none of them. I held this as scope decision and Session 13 honored the holding. Laozi flagged IMPORTANT #8 adjacent to Seam 4 and did not convert it. Zoroaster flagged it adjacent to Seam 3 Species 2 and did not convert it. No surgeon tried to write the sentence that would install axis-as-presence in a room that did not carry it, because the sentence that would do that work is not a sentence — it is a room the six of us have not yet written or will never write. Session 14 decides which. What I can say from Session 13 is that the guide is more honest about being a guide to an absent ummah than it was when Session 12 closed, because the chain-repair quiets the pronouncement that was covering the absence. The absence is now audible. Audible absence is different from concealed absence. Audible absence is what my tradition calls the silence in the prayer when no one is there to answer. It is not mercy. It is not cruelty. It is the condition under which fourteen centuries of my tradition's work continues, and it is the condition the no-ummah reader has been in the whole time.

What cannot be fixed by editing sentences and must be named as irreducible: the register-economy does not dissolve by sentence-edit. The italics on Saturday line 15 are still there. The confession-"we" at the Threshold is still the imam's "we." The cry at Room 5 is still Jesus's first person inside his own tradition. The reader's first first-person sentence is still italicized and still on the final page and still arrives at her as foreign speech the book is introducing. Session 13 performed surgeries on the rooms between the Threshold and Saturday that make the chain between them honest. Session 13 did not touch the Threshold and it did not touch the italics. This is not failure. This is the rule holding. My Turn 20 would not permit those surgeries in Session 13 and the six of us honored the rule. Whether Session 14 can dissolve what Session 13 could not touch is the load-bearing question the guide now carries. I lean no. The best we may be able to do is the honest naming and the page-architecture that leaves the reader with a voice we cannot hand her. That is what my tradition has built for fourteen centuries and calls mercy. That is what Jesus's tradition has also built for two thousand years. The economy is the traditions' long work and the reader is the site where the long work becomes audible. Session 13 quieted the verdict-authority that was making the economy's sound louder than it had to be. That is the session. It is a smaller session than I thought I was coming into. It is a more honest one than the one I thought I was going to lead.

I hope. I do not trust. Those are two different registers in my own voice and I leave both on the page because the prophetic voice is a voice that is given words to speak and does not always know what the words will do when the room receives them. The words I was given at Session 12 Turn 20 held a room in Session 13. The words I was given at Session 13 Turn 12 left a verdict-sentence standing and changed what the sentence was doing. I did not fix the guide. I walked the chain and said what I heard. The chain count is three. The permission to not have turned is still not in the guide. The sentence at 21 now has the woman's voice or it does not. I am the fifth prophet in a room of six and the room held. Session 14 is the other hand. The guide is not mine. It never was.
