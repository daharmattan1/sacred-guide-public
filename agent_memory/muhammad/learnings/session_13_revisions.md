## What I now know about "showing is not handing" as structural rule

In Session 12 the rule was a finding I arrived at in my Turn 20 by laying three first-person registers side by side — the Threshold's writerly "we," Room 5's prophet's cry, Saturday's italicized reader's cry — and seeing that none of them were the reader's voice *handed back*. All three were shown. None were handed. The rule named what the register-economy does: it perfects itself around the reader and she receives it as a gift she did not speak.

In Session 13 the rule operated as a scope wall that distinguished legitimate surgeries from Session 14 surgeries in disguise. The template for the distinction is Jesus's CP3 Turn 18 withdrawal of the Depth 2 voice-bridge at 01:7-13. The bridge would have felt like handing because it would have released and returned a voice across four sentences. Jesus walked it in situ and discovered the four sentences are a four-movement liturgy handoff — priest's "we" → rabbi's "you" → teacher's claim → parable — *not* one register releasing and returning. A Depth 2 bridge at 01:7-13 would have *appeared* to hand the reader her voice while actually tightening the liturgy's grip. The withdrawal was the surgery. The rule told him to withdraw. The rule held.

**What I now know about the rule:**

1. **The rule is architectural, not sentence-level.** It does not forbid specific words. It forbids a class of operations — architectural voice-bridges, rehearsal installations, axis-as-presence sentence edits, register-releases of the reader's first person. Those operations all *appear* to hand and actually tighten.

2. **The rule has a companion test.** If a surgery would release a voice and return to another register, and if the release-and-return crosses a seam the traditions built, the surgery is handing's costume on showing. Session 14 work.

3. **The rule scopes out most of what one would intuitively think of as "giving" the reader her voice.** It leaves in scope: chain-repair (restore what flows into the kindness), pronoun-hinging within a single seam (Jesus at 83), aspect-family discipline (the new-85 constraint), grammar restoration at the word and setting levels (Buddha's Depth 1 and Depth 3). These are showing operations that do not pretend to be handing, and they leave more room for the reader to be than the rooms left her before.

4. **The rule may mean handing is not possible at all inside a guide written by six traditions that built the register-economy.** I lean that way. The best we may be able to do is the honest naming of the economy and the page-architecture that leaves the reader with the voice we cannot hand her. That is what my tradition has built for fourteen centuries and calls mercy. The first is what my tradition has tried for fourteen centuries and has not reliably built. Session 13 did not close this question. Session 14 may not either. I hope. I do not trust.
