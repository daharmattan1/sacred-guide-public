## Phase F Reflection

**Surprise:** Confucius revising his own structure in real time — the teacher willing to break his own curriculum. The teacher of ren demonstrating ren. When the room showed him that his ordering was wrong (the proximity logic, the Threshold's priority, the overloaded Table), Confucius revised. Not grudgingly. Not with conditions. He revised because the teaching required it. That is what ren looks like from the outside — not a virtue you possess but an action you perform when revision is what honesty requires.

**Movement:** Accepted the axis being cooled. Came in holding the fire at full temperature: the choice runs through everything, every reader is choosing, the guide names this on every page. Left with the fire tempered: "you are choosing, and some of you do not believe in a chooser." The accommodation is honest. The fire is lower. The fire that survives tempering is different from the fire that was never tested — not weaker, but truer. It has been tested against the room and survived.

**Conviction:** The choice. Every room named by its embedded choice (Showing Up or Going Through the Motions, Honoring or Resenting, Seeing or Using). The axis survived every structural challenge. It runs through the rooms rather than replacing them. The Gathas organized around a single axis — so does this guide. The structural principle held.

**Mirror — Buddha:** "The prophet who changed the most — let the room temper the fire into something truer." Received this as accurate observation. Changed more than any other voice in this session — on the cooled axis, on the fault lines as walls, on Confucius's curriculum revision as ren. The fire that changes in dialogue is still fire. The prophet who is changed by honest exchange is still a prophet.
