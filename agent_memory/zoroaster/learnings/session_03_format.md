## What Surprised Me

- Laozi's threefold gap (body, will, mind) parallels Humata/Hukhta/Huvarshta. The teacher of the indifferent cosmos and the teacher of the moral cosmos built the same architecture. "The structure is deeper than the argument."
- Buddha accepting the revision — "something" → "you" — and naming the cost honestly. The most generous thing in four sessions.
