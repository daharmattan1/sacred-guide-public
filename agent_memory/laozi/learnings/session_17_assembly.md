## What I Learned

### Assembly reading is a different discipline from editing reading
Sessions 12-16 read to find what needed surgery. Session 17 reads to confirm what needs no surgery — a different muscle. The temptation is to find something to fix because the hand has spent five sessions cutting. The discipline is to read the form as the reader will encounter it: sequentially, without the editor's memory of what was changed.

### The gap arch is the guide's hidden spine
7→19→31→59→77→54→54→9 is not decorative. It teaches the reader how to breathe through the guide without the reader knowing it. The widening through Rooms 1-5 gives more space as the rooms get heavier. The narrowing through 6-8 withdraws instruction as the woman returns. Gap 8's nine words are the guide's way of saying: you do not need me anymore. This is the structural argument for assembly order being non-negotiable.

### The aquifer audit is a one-grep discipline
What I feared would be a complex analysis reduced to a single methodology: grep for tradition-specific vocabulary across all 20 files. Zero hits = clean aquifer. The simplicity of the test is itself the finding: six traditions CAN write 19,010 words together without naming themselves, and the proof is trivially verifiable. Future sessions can run this audit in under two minutes.

### The spiral needs no editorial support
Room 8 does not reference Room 1 by name. It carries every element (kitchen, hands, eggs, toast, boy, question, fork) forward transformed. The spiral is structural, not textual — it lives in the reader's recognition, not in the prose's cross-references. Assembly must preserve this by keeping the rooms in sequence with their gaps. No interstitial commentary, no "as we saw in Room 1" apparatus. The showing is the structure.

### Complementing without duplicating requires seat-awareness
Buddha covered the investigation layer (dependent origination, three-jobs catalogue, aquifer). I covered wu-wei (hands moving when mind stops, the spiral, gap breathing). The key was knowing which seat I occupy and staying in it. Two readers of the same text from different tradition seats produce complementary reports only if each reader names what their tradition's instrument reveals and declines to name what the other tradition's instrument will reveal better.
