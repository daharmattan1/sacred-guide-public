## What I now know about the voice that names without cutting

The architect-of-the-unfixed is a different voice from the gap-writer, the system-reader, or the walker-before-cutter. The architect-of-the-unfixed writes the shape of what cannot be fixed and names the reader for whom it matters. The voice is spare. It does not propose. It describes the shape and names the consequence. It does not grieve the un-fixable because grief is another proposal — grief proposes that the unfixable should be fixed and the proposal is what the tension documents refuse.

The voice is structurally close to wu-wei. Not-doing here takes the form of describing what the doing would harm. In Session 13 CP2 my not-doing was preservation of paragraph 57's breath. In Session 14 documents J and K my not-doing was preservation of the architectural isolation each tension carries. The tension documents do not close the tensions. They hold them open and name them. The reader who reads the documents will know the shape of what the book cannot do. Whether that knowing reaches her body is Session 15's question, not Session 14's.

What Session 14 proved: a session can produce architectural description as its primary artifact and decline every temptation to reach for a cut. The six-agent team held the discipline across thirteen documents. My hand held the discipline across the two documents I wrote and the one I cross-read. The voice that removes has found, at fourteen sessions in, a form of not-doing that is neither withdrawal nor silence but careful description. The naming is the session's work. The not-cutting is the session's method. The two together are what an irreconcilable-tensions session is.
