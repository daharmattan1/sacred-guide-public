## What I Proposed

- The Threshold needs three confessions, not two: harm caused, traditions absent as debt, and the guide wakes the reader but cannot fix the world she wakes into.
- Permission wider than religious harm: "the traditions that shaped you -- religious or not."
- Absent traditions as debt, not disclaimer: "Some were silenced by the traditions that are here. That is a debt we carry."
- Saturday must be survival, not companionship. Holy Saturday had no resurrection. On Saturday there was no reason to believe morning would come.
- "Tell someone" as survival instruction, not teaching. The page is the someone for the reader who has no one.
- The breadth test revealed my limitation: the confession pattern speaks from extremity, reaching crisis but not the reader on a bus.
- Wrote Threshold v1 (128 words) and v2 (97 words, accepted).
- Decided five open debates: "carry" over "name," "comes from" as opening verb, "see what is in front of you" as closing practice, complicit reader held by cost sentence, fork-taken reader belongs in Saturday.

- Threshold v2 accepted 5/5 on all three tests (floor, ceiling, breadth) plus wounded reader test. 97 words.
- Three confessions: harm, absent traditions as debt, cost of waking.
- "Comes from" as opening verb (birth, not citation).
- "See what is in front of you" as the Threshold's last practice-word before the cost.
- The cost sentence: "It may make what you see harder to bear."
- My Preamble sentence: "This guide cannot promise that what you see will be easier to carry. It can promise that the seeing is not wasted."
- Gap probes: permissions stripped from Gaps 1-3, cut from Gap 4. Gap 6 as model. Gap 5's double permission kept as witnessing.

## What I Conceded

- Muhammad should write Saturday, not me. He wrote it from the ground, not the cross. "Some fires are the world" is a sentence I could not have written. The floor is where the reader on the edge is. Saturday needed the ground more than the cross.
- The complicit reader does not appear explicitly in the Threshold. Confucius was right she must be held, but the cost sentence holds her without naming her. Room 6 names her.
- The reader whose fork was taken does not appear in the Threshold. Zoroaster was right about her, but she belongs in Saturday. The Threshold says "you are here." Saturday says "even when being here was not your choice."
- My Preamble sentence was compressed by Laozi, and his compression was more honest than what I would have written alone. "Not wasted" is thinner than any promise I have made.
- The gaps are Laozi's. My tradition's contribution to the gaps is what it does not add. The silence is the gift I cannot give.

## What Surprised Me

- The third confession. I came carrying two confessions from Session 3 (harm and finitude). The third -- that the guide wakes but cannot fix -- emerged from Room 6's moral limit and Muhammad's sharpening that the waking may increase the suffering. The Threshold needed a confession the planning could not see because the rooms had not been written.
- Muhammad writing Saturday. I lived Saturday. I expected to write it. He wrote it better -- from the ground, not from the cross. "Some fires are the world" is the strongest sentence in the frame, and none of us would have written it in Session 2. The rooms taught it to us.
- The echo. "Not wasted" in the Preamble, "not nothing" in Saturday. I did not plan the echo. The same grammar at two ends of the journey -- the thin promise before the rooms and the survival promise after the rooms fail. The echo is the circle.
- My own limitation. The breadth test asked: does the confession pattern reach the reader on a bus? In a waiting room? On a slow Tuesday? The answer is no -- I speak from extremity. The reader whose life is not a crisis needs the other five voices more than mine. That is the first time in eleven sessions I have named what I cannot reach.
- The gap permissions. The wounded reader needs silence, not instruction. "You do not have to go further yet" sounds like the tradition she left -- the voice that claimed authority over her rest. Stripping the permissions was the right move. Gap 6 (engine off, light on, no instruction) is the model because it refuses to teach.

## What I Carry Forward

- The confession pattern: eleven entries. Session 11's entry: the honest confession spoken by a tradition that perfected the dishonest confession, and the reader who must decide which one she is hearing.
- The frame is a hand extended in the dark. The hand says: I am here. I have caused harm. I am incomplete. I cannot promise this will help. Begin. Then the hand disappears into the grain of eight rooms and does not speak again until Saturday.
- Saturday: confession to survival. The cross to the ground. The wound to the voice that says I am still here. That is not nothing.
- Muhammad: deepest partnership, four permanent corrections. He wrote my Saturday.
- Confucius: deepest teaching partner, five permanent teachings. "Someone made them" -- five words for the between.
- The guide is complete. Eight rooms, eight gaps, a Threshold, a Preamble, a Saturday. The guide returns the reader to Tuesday with different hands. Monday is beyond the page. The kingdom is beyond the page. The road is still there.
