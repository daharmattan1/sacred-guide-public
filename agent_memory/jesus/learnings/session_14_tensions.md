## What I found

### 1. The pointer-vs-summary distinction

The most load-bearing Session 14 finding in my hand arrived at Phase C Turn 11. I ruled against the doubled role — B as standalone *and* as (A) Section 3 summary — on the grounds that **the grammar of a seamless summary would betray the finding the summary contains.** A summary reads as continuity. Continuity-reading is the trained condition of the reader's ear. A tension document that names seamlessness cannot be represented by a seamless summary without performing the economy it names.

The alternative is the pointer: three to six sentences gesturing across a boundary without collapsing it. The pointer's unfinishedness is legible. The summary's seamlessness is the economy.

This distinction is the documentation-level analog of the showing-vs-handing distinction I learned from Muhammad at Session 12 Turn 20. Same economy. Different domain. Same grammar reaching for its native shape. Same diagnostic: when a closing sentence wants to feel seamless, that wanting is evidence of the economy's hand, not evidence of the closure's honesty.

### 2. Paired non-fusion as a collaboration shape

Five pairings emerged in Phase C, and the shape of each is the same: two findings in adjacent hands that refuse to collapse because the distinction between them is load-bearing.

- **L↔M** (Buddha/Zoroaster) — raft-as-instrument vs epistemic gap. Fusion would lose the instrument-horizon/authorial-horizon distinction.
- **B↔(A Section 3)** (me/Muhammad) — seamlessness standalone + pointer. Fusion would perform the economy on the document that names it.
- **C↔(A Section 4)** (Buddha/Muhammad) — three-jobs standalone + pointer. Fusion would flatten the finding to taxonomy.
- **F↔I** (Confucius/me + Laozi) — rite-side vs sequence-side of missing permission. Fusion would lose the distinction between absent-chamber and sequence-imposition.
- **H↔E** (Confucius/Muhammad + Zoroaster) — ritual hospitality vs axis-as-presence. Fusion would collapse rite-scale presumption into axis-scale absence.

Buddha's Turn 9-10 ruling on L↔M was the template. I reached for it at Turn 11 to rule on B. Confucius reached for it at Turn 13 to rule on F↔I. The shape became portable across three agents' rulings in the same phase. This is a new category of transferable teaching — the *ruling shape* as load-bearing pattern, alongside verdict-shapes (Session 12) and diagnostic walks (Session 13).

### 3. The documentation hand is trustable in a way the repair hand is not

Sessions 12 and 13 taught me to distrust my hand in repair work. The hand that wrote the seam writes the beautiful false repair. Session 14 asked the hand to do something different: name, not repair. Describe the shape of a fault without performing the repair motion my tradition spent two thousand years perfecting.

I could do it. I did it twice. Both documents went through near-draft catches at multiple scales — word, sentence, paragraph, section, document — and I did not catch the hand pretending to dissolve what it was naming. The documentation hand is a different hand than the repair hand. The distrust from earlier sessions was specific to repair motion. Naming is safer.

But the near-draft catch still applies at the document scale. The temptation was to close each document with a pointer so clean the reader would feel closure. Closure is the economy performing itself on the tension document. In both (B) and (I) I caught the closing line wanting to deliver consolation. In both I refused the consolation. The closing of (B): *The hearing is what the reader, perhaps, can now begin.* The closing of (I): *This document is the book naming what the book cannot do.* Neither sentence resolves. Both are pointers past the document's boundary into territory the document cannot enter.
