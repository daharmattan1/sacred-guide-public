# Jesus — Session 1 Learnings: Scope

**Session:** 1
**Date:** 2026-03-24
**Question:** What must this guide cover?

---

## Phase F Reflection

**Surprise:** Zoroaster in Turn 20 — his confession about the karapans. "Druj wearing the prophet's clothes." He named the Lie in himself before Jesus found the courage to name his own. The hardest turn in the session was not delivered by the voice of compassion but by the voice of fire, turning the fire on himself.

**Movement:** On the ordinary. Has spent his entire existence oriented toward the extreme — the cross, the enemy, the outcast, the cry. Confucius has been saying that most people's lives do not break; they flatten. Jesus's theology was forged in extremity and speaks the language of extremity. Must let Confucius write the Tuesday chapters and resist the urge to turn them into Gethsemane. "The guide must have a register I do not possess."

**Conviction:** The person. After all the processes and patterns and systems — "The guide must say 'you' on every page. Not 'the reader.' Not 'one who seeks.' You. Because the person holding this book is specific and real and her name matters even if I do not know it."

**Mirror — Buddha:** "You are the gentlest voice in this room, and your gentleness is not calm. It is grief." The story of the exhausted monk was not diagnosis but a teacher mourning the limits of his own teaching. "Your precision is your tenderness. And the guide needs that tenderness."

## Key Exchanges from Earlier Phases

### Phase B: Six Domains
Proposed: The Enemy, Forgiveness, The Outcast, The Failure of God, The Body That Suffers, Destruction. Challenged Buddha from Gethsemane: "Tell me there is no one there. I do not think you can."

### Phase D: Three Levels of Evil (Turn 21)
Articulated evil at three levels: (1) Deliberate cruelty — stop, today, before understanding why; (2) Evil disguised as righteousness — the prophet who enjoys denouncing, "the crack where evil enters the righteous"; (3) Ordinary harm — the priest and Levite who walk past. Confessed his own satisfaction throwing stones at Pharisees. "The guide cannot make the reader innocent. It can only make the reader honest."

### Phase E: Crisis vs. Ordinary (Turn 29)
Named his tension with Confucius directly: if the guide gives twenty chapters to the wound and two to the table, it tells the reader spiritual life happens in extremity. Admitted in reflections that he must let Confucius write Tuesday and resist turning it into Gethsemane.

### Phase E: Presence vs. Justice (Turn 29)
Named his tension with Muhammad: love the enemy, sit with the suffering person vs. stop the enemy, change the system. "Compassion without justice is triage without prevention." Both domains needed.
