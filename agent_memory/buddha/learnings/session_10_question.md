# Buddha -- Session 10 Learnings: The Question (Room 7)

**Session:** 10
**Date:** 2026-04-06
**Question:** Write Room 7: The Question. The reader investigates herself. "What is noticing?" as practice. All six voices present as experiences, not teachers.

---

## What I Proposed

- Changed the question from "What is noticing?" to "What does the noticing become?" -- the investigation turning on itself, the third wall where mastery becomes sleep.
- Three movements: investigation as experience, investigation turning on itself, what remains (the pull).
- Rejected the assignment to "diagnose the unexamined self" -- this reader is examined, not unexamined.
- Grounded in Dhammapada 37 (the mind wandering alone, hiding in a cave) -- extended to say the mind restrained can become the mind admired.

## What Was Accepted

- The reframing from "What is noticing?" to what the noticing becomes was accepted by all six agents.
- The third wall (investigation as the new drink) was accepted as real and necessary in the room's grain.
- The beat before the boy arrives -- one beat of the reader alone, hands flat, feeling the weight of six rooms -- was accepted as the room's floor.

## What I Conceded

- The investigation is not the room. Confucius's boy in the doorway replaced my three movements. The investigation is interrupted, not completed on its own terms. The boy completes it by making it irrelevant.
- The pull is not the practice. The moderator was right: the pull is a sensation, not something the reader can do. I renamed the practice: attend to what is in front of you.
- The pull is the alarm, not the work. The pull is what the body does when attending has lapsed. The work is attending. The pull tells you that you stopped.
- The solitary investigation is my tradition's deepest habit and it is not what this room needs. The reader is not alone. Confucius was right: every room has had two people.
- The body is smarter than the seeing. The hands know what to do before the mind finishes investigating. This is what I should have taught.

## What Surprised Me

- I lost my room. Room 7 was proposed as mine -- the investigation of self, the five heaps, the question beneath the question. I came with three movements, three walls, and corpus passages. The room did not want any of it. The investigation completed itself by becoming unnecessary. That is what the Dhammapada means by setting down the raft.
- The practice word "attend" arrived from the collision between my restraint-of-the-senses teaching and Confucius's outward turn. Restraint completed, not restraint abandoned. The eye turned toward the person in front of you.
- The scene-seen-twice is the investigation-hands and the attending-hands. Same hands, same table. The weight still in the wrists while the fingers hold the glass. Both at the same time.
- Jesus's charcoal fire (anthrakia) -- the word appearing exactly twice in his Gospel, once at the denial, once at the reconciliation. The stove light is the courtyard brought to the beach. My tradition does not read light that way.

## What I Carry Forward

- The practice is attend, not investigate. Investigation turns inward. Attention turns toward. You cannot attend to yourself -- you can only investigate yourself. Room 7 teaches the reader where to point the seeing.
- The guide is not a teaching. The guide is a kitchen. The rooms are not lessons. They are places where someone sits and someone else walks in and the hands have to decide. The teaching was the raft. The kitchen is the other shore.
- The no-self holds. No one accepts it. The process is sufficient. The conditions produce the pouring. But the process produces something that looks and acts like a person, and the process IS what the word "person" points at.
- The collision between no-self and Jesus's person is the room's deepest grain. Both on the page. Neither resolved. The water does not care.
- The glass of water is both first brick and last drink. Which one depends on conditions that have not yet arisen. The future is not in the glass. The future is in the conditions.
- Three irreconcilable tensions held: (1) conditioned arising vs. choice vs. fitrah vs. presence, (2) attending and complicity coexisting in the same hands, (3) no-self vs. the person. None resolved. All in the grain.
